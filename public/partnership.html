The Agreement

CNK Booths, LLC — Venue Photo Booth Partnership
  • Revenue share. The venue keeps 25% of the gross revenue the booth collects; CNK Booths keeps 75%.
  • Payouts. CNK Booths collects all booth revenue and pays the venue its 25% each month, between the 1st and 5th.
  • Term. A 90-day initial term, then continues month-to-month. Either party may cancel with 30 days' written notice.
  • Exclusivity. During the agreement, the venue will not host any other or competing photo booth.
  • Zero cost to the venue. CNK Booths owns the booth and handles installation, maintenance, repairs, restocking, and software at no cost to the venue.
  • Venue provides. Floor space, electrical power, and Wi-Fi for the booth. On termination, CNK Booths removes the booth.
